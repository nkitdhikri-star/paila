Destinations

Places we know well enough to send you there

Infinite journeys across Nepal — from the tea hills of Ilam to the ice walls of Annapurna Base Camp. Pick one and we'll build the rest of the trip around it.

Kathmandu

Kathmandu Valley (Pashupatinath World Heritage Site)

Nepal

Nepal's capital is a living museum. Three Durbar Squares — Kathmandu, Patan and Bhaktapur — sit within an hour of each other, wrapped around the great stupas of Swayambhunath and Boudhanath and the old brick lanes of Thamel.

All year
Pokhara

Pokhara

Nepal

Painted wooden boats on Phewa Lake, the Annapurna range mirrored in still morning water, and a lakeside strip built for slow evenings. Sunrise from Sarangkot, paragliding and the World Peace Pagoda are all a short ride away.

Sep – Apr
Annapurna

Annapurna Base Camp

Nepal

A trek straight into the Annapurna sanctuary at 4,130 m. Rhododendron forest and Gurung villages give way to a ring of ice walls, and one morning you wake to Annapurna I turning gold. Teahouse lodging the whole way.

Mar – May, Oct – Nov
Mustang

Mustang

Nepal

The dry, wind-carved kingdom behind the Himalaya, where ochre cliffs and whitewashed monasteries sit under skies that rarely cloud over. Kagbeni, the Muktinath temple and the walled city of Lo Manthang.

Mar – Nov
Bajura

Badimalika

The Switzerland of Nepal

Far-western Nepal's best-kept secret, and the reason people call it the Switzerland of Nepal. Green meadows roll to the horizon at around 4,200 m, and the Badimalika temple draws pilgrims every Janai Purnima.

Jul – Sep
Ilam

Ilam Tea Gardens

Nepal

Tea gardens rolling away to the horizon in eastern Nepal, with mist sitting in the valleys until mid-morning. Sunrise from Antu Danda, a walk through the Kanyam estates, and a cup picked from the hill you're standing on.

All year
Lumbini

Lumbini

Birthplace of Lord Buddha

The birthplace of Lord Buddha and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Maya Devi Temple, the Ashoka pillar and the sacred pond sit inside a quiet monastic zone where countries from across the Buddhist world have each built a monastery.

Oct – Mar
Rasuwa

Gosaikunda Lake

Nepal

A sacred alpine lake at 4,380 m in the Langtang range, frozen for much of the winter and ringed by snow. It is a short, steep trek from Dhunche, and every Janai Purnima the shoreline fills with pilgrims.

Mar – May, Sep – Oct
Chitwan

Chitwan National Park

Nepal

Nepal's first national park and the best place in the country to meet a one-horned rhino. Jeep and canoe safaris through elephant grass and sal forest, a Tharu stick dance in the evening, and sunsets over the Rapti river.

Oct – Mar
Tanahun

Bandipur

Nepal

A hilltop Newari bazaar frozen in the 18th century — cobbled lanes, carved windows and pastel townhouses with no cars in sight. Climb to Thani Mai temple at sunrise for a wall of Himalayan peaks from Manaslu to the Annapurnas.

All year
Bardiya

Bardiya National Park

Nepal

Nepal's largest national park, and a quieter alternative to Chitwan for spotting wild tigers, elephants and gharial along the Karnali River. Jeep and canoe safaris through sal forest and grassland, with evenings spent in Tharu villages.

Oct – Mar
Kaski

Ghandruk

Nepal

A stone-paved Gurung village terraced into the Annapurna foothills, with Machhapuchhre and Annapurna South filling the skyline from almost every doorway. One of the most rewarding short treks in the region, with teahouses along the way.

Oct – May
Gorkha

Gorkha

Nepal

The hill town where Prithvi Narayan Shah began the campaign that unified Nepal. Gorkha Durbar's whitewashed palace sits on a ridge above the valley, with Manaslu and Himalchuli filling the horizon on a clear day.

All year
Morang

Jefale

Nepal

A community-homestay hill village tucked into the same district Paila Travel calls home. Climb the Jephale view tower for a rare low-altitude sighting of both Kanchenjunga and Everest, then settle into a family-run homestay for the night.

Oct – Apr
Dolakha

Jiri

Nepal

Known as the "Switzerland of Nepal" for its rolling green hills and cool climate, Jiri was the original trailhead for Everest expeditions before the Lukla airstrip existed. Sherpa villages and pine forest, with a slower start to the Everest trail.

Oct – Apr
Dolakha

Kalinchowk (Kuri Village)

Nepal

Home base for the Kalinchowk Bhagwati temple, perched near 3,800 m and reached by a short hike or a cable car ride above the clouds. Winter buries the ridge in snow; on a clear day the view runs across Gaurishankar, Langtang and Ganesh Himal.

Nov – Feb
Sunsari

Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve

Nepal

Nepal's most important wetland, where the Sapta Koshi spreads into marsh and grassland sheltering the country's last wild water buffalo and more than 500 bird species. A paradise for birdwatchers, with dugout canoe rides through the reeds at dawn.

Oct – Mar
Manang

Manang

Nepal

A high, dry Himalayan valley on the Annapurna Circuit, where Tibetan-style villages sit under Annapurna II and Gangapurna. Old monasteries, Milarepa's cave and a built-in acclimatisation day — most trekkers pause here before crossing the Thorong La.

Mar – May, Sep – Nov
Dhankuta

Namje, Bhedetar

Nepal

A Magar village above the hill station of Bhedetar, once named one of CNN's most underrated destinations in the world. Wide views of the eastern Himalaya, traditional Magar dance and dress, and cool weather a world away from the Terai plains below.

All year
Taplejung

Pathibhara Temple

Nepal

One of Nepal's most revered Shakti Peeth shrines, perched above 3,700 m in Taplejung with Kanchenjunga rising behind it. A cable car now carries pilgrims and trekkers most of the way up, with the final stretch on foot through rhododendron forest.

Mar – May, Sep – Nov
Dolakha

Sailung

Nepal

A grassy ridge on the Dolakha–Ramechhap border that opens into a full 360-degree horizon — Gaurishankar, Langtang, Ganesh Himal and, on the clearest days, Everest itself. Hindu and Buddhist shrines share the summit among a hundred rolling hillocks.

Oct – May
