About Paila

First step of your journey, taken with someone local

Paila Travel & Experiences started as a physical travel desk in Sundar Haraincha, Morang — now we're bringing that same hands-on planning online.

Our story

Built on local footpaths, not a franchise map

Paila Travel & Experiences ("Paila" means "step" — as in the first step of a journey) began as a walk-in travel desk in Sundar Haraincha, Morang, helping neighbours and local travellers plan trips across Nepal — from the tea gardens of Ilam to the jungles of Chitwan and the lakes of Pokhara.

We're now building this website so anyone, anywhere, can plan and book a trip with us the same way our walk-in customers always have — with a real conversation, not just a form.

Where we're based

Sundar Haraincha, Morang

Being based in Morang keeps us close to eastern Nepal's routes and guides, while our packages reach destinations across the country. Reach out any time — by phone, WhatsApp, or the booking form on this site.

Opening the travel desk
